## FAQ

### Is this a compatibility score?

No. The report explores the relationship’s patterns, resources, and growth edges without reducing two people to a percentage or verdict.

### What if one or both birth times are unknown?

You may still order. Unknown-time angles and houses are omitted, and that person’s noon-reference Moon is excluded from ranked aspects and composite interpretation. The PDF states the reduced precision clearly.

### Can this be used for friendship or family?

Yes. The customer selects the relationship context, and the report avoids romantic or sexual assumptions when that context does not fit.

### Is the map included with the report?

The report includes a visual Top 3 map page. The standalone map listing provides five poster-size, one-page files designed specifically for wall printing.

### Will I receive a physical item?

No. Both products are personalized digital PDFs. No frame or physical print is included.

### Does the purchase create a Kairos account or subscription?

No. Etsy fulfillment provides only the files promised in the purchased listing. It does not create an account, product entitlement, subscription, or marketing enrollment.

### How is my information used?

Birth details are used only to calculate and fulfill the personalized product. The buyer’s Etsy order identity and support email are kept out of the AI interpretation prompt. Final retention language must be approved before launch and must match the implemented deletion process.
